About KADIMA

We build AI for one job: find the customer’s problem and resolve it, without making them wait, repeat themselves, or lose trust.

Why we exist

Customers do not have ticket problems. They have life problems.

A payment that failed. An order that never moved. A policy no one can explain the same way twice. Those are the problems. The queue, the chatbot, and the hold music are just where they show up.

KADIMA is an AI company built around that distinction. We connect what the customer said, what your systems already know, and what should happen next — then we close the loop, or hand a person a case that is already understood.

What we measure

Problems resolved on first contact
Issues that come back
Customers saved before they cancel

Not tickets opened. Not messages sent.

Mission

Make the customer’s problem disappear

Fast replies are easy. A resolved customer is the product. Every system we ship is judged on whether the person on the other side is done.

For customers

An answer in their words, on the channel they chose, without starting over.

For teams

Fewer repeated cases, clearer causes, and time for the problems that need a person.

For the business

Retention you can see, and answers you can defend.

How we work

Start with the problem, then the model

We do not drop a general chatbot on a help centre. We map the problems customers actually have, ground the AI in your knowledge, and only automate what it can finish.

Name the problems

Cluster contacts, cancellations, and repeats until the real issues are obvious.

Resolve what is known

Answer from approved knowledge, with the source attached, in your voice.

Hand off what is not

When a person is needed, they inherit the context. The customer does not retell it.

Fix the cause

Repeats become a product signal, not another shift of tickets.

Outcomes we design for

Governance

Trust is part of the answer

A customer problem is not resolved if the reply is wrong, off-policy, or careless with their data. We design for that from the start.

Rules before generation

Brand voice, refund limits, and privacy boundaries are constraints, not a review after the fact.
